AFRIPULSE AI 🌍

Africa's Living Intelligence & Innovation Network. A bilingual (English/Hausa) platform where communities across Nigeria report local problems — roads, water, health — and AI-assisted analysis helps surface and cluster them for decision-makers. Geographic data covers all 36 states and the FCT (774 LGAs, 8,700+ verified wards); the community pilot is anchored in Rafi LGA, Niger State. Every AI assessment is labelled a suggestion pending human review.
